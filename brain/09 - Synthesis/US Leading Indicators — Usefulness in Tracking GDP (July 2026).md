# US Leading Indicators — Usefulness in Tracking GDP (July 2026)

**What it is** — An Excel workbook operationalizing the published **"Usefulness in tracking GDP"** top-10 ranking (Goldman Sachs research; intrinsic values 2.92 → 0.69): Philadelphia Fed index, initial claims, UMich confidence (prelim & final), Chicago PMI, nonfarm payrolls, unemployment, capacity utilization, ISM PMI, retail sales ex-autos. Each indicator gets **full free-data history** (~9,200 observations; claims weekly to 1967, payrolls to 1939, ISM to 1948), the Analysis-3.0 statistical layer (change transform, 12-bin histogram + probabilities, descriptive stats with kurtosis/skew reads, ±1/2/3σ bands, actual-vs-normal, **Z-analysis of the latest print**) and 3 charts per sheet (27 charts). The Dashboard replicates the ranking with live z/percentile columns and an economy read per indicator.

**Honest substitutions (disclosed on-sheet):** Chicago PMI is proprietary (MNI) → CFNAI shown as a labeled free substitute; the UMich *preliminary* vintage is not freely archived → the final series serves both ranks 3 and 9; ISM comes from the maintained local full-history file (FRED's NAPM is discontinued). Claims and unemployment are inverted for the economy read; payrolls z-scored on the monthly change; retail ex-autos on YoY.
