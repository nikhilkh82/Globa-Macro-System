# Trading System — Backtest (June 2026)

**Live dashboard:** [Macro Trend System — Dashboard](../../trading%20system/Macro%20Trend%20System%20-%20Dashboard.html) (the launcher's "Cross-Asset Trend (flagship)" card)

**What it is & why it matters** — A systematic, *backtestable* distillation of the [[The Global Macros Framework|Global Macros framework]] into a cross-asset **long/short** rule, run on **real price history** and **adversarially audited twice**. It answers "how strong is the mechanical version of this method?" honestly, with full edge-attribution. Artifacts in project-root `trading system/` (interactive dashboard + Excel), generated by `tools/build_trading_system.py`. **No fabricated performance — every figure is computed and exactly reproducible.**

## Framework → rule
| Framework layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| Macro direction / "path of least resistance" | **Magnitude-weighted trend** = risk-adjusted 6/12m momentum, capped ±1 ([[Technical Analysis & Price Action]]) |
| Exogenous driver (FX) | **Carry** = 3m interbank rate diff vs USD, 30% of FX signal ([[Endo-Exo Toolkit & Workflow]]) |
| Risk layer | **Inverse-vol, equal risk per cluster** (Equity/Rates/FX/Commodity) — [[Average True Range (ATR)]] / [[Risk Management]] |
| Long/short book | Continuous long/short, vol-targeted ([[Portfolio Management]]) |

**Universe (23 / 4 clusters):** 3 equity indices · 3 US bonds (2Y/10Y/30Y, **synthetic constant-maturity total return** from yields) · 11 FX (EUR/GBP/AUD/JPY/CAD/CHF/NZD/SEK/NOK/MXN/ZAR) · 6 commodities (WTI/Brent/NatGas/Copper/**Gold/Silver**; gold & silver from Yahoo `GC=F`/`SI=F` — FRED feeds discontinued).
**Discipline:** monthly rebalance · **no look-ahead** (position *t-1* earns *t*) · 10bps cost · growing universe (S&P/Dow join 2016).

## Edge attribution (the honest story)
| Variant / benchmark | Sharpe |
|---|---|
| **Full system** (long/short, 4 clusters) | **0.68** |
| minus Rates sleeve (no bonds) | 0.45 (MaxDD −19.7%) |
| long-only signals (shorts zeroed) | 1.00 (MaxDD −7.8%) |
| Benchmark — equal-weight long-only | 0.56 |
| Benchmark — matched signed long/short | 0.14 |

Beats both the naive long-only (0.56) **and** the structure-matched signed L/S benchmark (0.14) → the **cluster-vol sizing + magnitude weighting add real value**. But two caveats: (1) the **bond sleeve is a major driver** — ex-Rates Sharpe is **0.45**, below the long-only bench, so trend/carry on equities/FX/commodities alone doesn't beat passive; the per-instrument attribution says the same thing from the other side — cumulative contribution (`contribution`, % of capital over the full span) is led by **US 2Y +53.0%**, ahead of **NASDAQ +35.8%**, with **Copper +15.1%, Gold +11.6%, US 10Y +10.8%** behind and the FX book barely registering (worst: **CHF/USD −2.8%**); (2) **shorting is a net Sharpe drag** here (long-only-signals = 1.00), earning its keep only in 2008/2020 drawdown control. Universe broadened with hindsight + un-validated hyperparameters → realistic **forward Sharpe ~0.40–0.50**. The framework's directional edge stays **discretionary** ([[The Global Macros Framework|endo/exo]]). The dashboard's actionable LONG/SHORT/FLAT column is the 2026-05 formation described in the box above.

**FX carry coverage (`carry_cov`, `fx_cov_avg`).** The carry leg averages **90%** coverage across the 11 FX pairs: seven are at 100% (AUD, CAD, CHF, NZD, SEK, NOK, MXN), JPY/USD at 95% and EUR/USD and GBP/USD at 98%, and **ZAR/USD at 0%** — the rand contributes no carry at all, so its signal is trend-only over the whole backtest. That is the one structural hole in the FX sleeve and it is disclosed, not renormalised away quietly.

## Adversarial audits (two workflow passes, 2026-06-20)
**Verdict: presentable, genuine, no look-ahead, no fabrication.** Pass 1 (5 auditors) fixed a Sortino divisor bug, removed equity triple-counting (cluster sizing), disclosed carry coverage. Pass 2 (4 auditors + experiments) *empirically proved* the synthetic bond index is causal (identical levels rebuilt at 5 cutoffs), verified bond sign/duration math, and reproduced the attribution (ex-Rates 0.46, long-only 1.01, matched-L/S 0.15). Rejected false positives: carry/FX-inversion sign and a bond carry-look-ahead claim (the suggested "fix" would have *introduced* bias). A real bug fixed mid-build: negative interest rates (CHF/JPY 2015–22) were being dropped, suppressing carry coverage.
