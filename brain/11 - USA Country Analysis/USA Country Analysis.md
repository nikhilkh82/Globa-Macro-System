# USA Country Analysis — folder 11

**What it is** — USA country economic analysis (2016–2026) built entirely from official data via FRED (BEA/BLS/Fed/Treasury): a Word + PDF report (inline-cited) covering GDP, inflation, unemployment, policy rate and debt trajectories, **and** an interactive HTML dashboard version of the same. `tools/build_usa_country_analysis.py` — re-run again **2026-09-24 16:53**, rewriting the `.docx` (report date 24 Sep 2026) and the dashboard (its banner now reads "as of 2026-09-24"); the `.pdf` was **again** not regenerated and still carries the 2026-06-21 build. A week after the Fed's 17-Sep hike, both *still* print fed funds **3.63%**, because the builder reads the monthly `FEDFUNDS` (Aug) rather than the target range or the daily `DFF`, and the report still says the Fed "pivoted to gradual easing" with "a mild easing bias". Do not quote their policy-rate line.

**Current read — refreshed 2026-09-24 from the calendar-keyed FRED pack (`tools/macro_pack.json`, generated 2026-09-24 16:04)**: a late-cycle expansion in which **the hike has stuck and the energy shock has half-unwound, but the long end has not come back down**. The Fed's **first hike of the cycle** — 25bp to a **3.75–4.00%** target range — took effect **17-Sep** and has held every day since: daily fed funds (`DFF`) prints **3.88%** from 17-Sep through **22-Sep**, against **3.63%** on every day through 16-Sep and 3.63% for monthly August (`FEDFUNDS`). Energy is the week's real change: **WTI $96.41** and **Brent $114.89** (both 22-Sep) are **off** the 15-Sep spike highs of $107.02 / $130.80 that the 19-Sep read carried — a $10.61 / $15.91 give-back in five sessions — yet still **+53.1%** and **+71.8%** y/y. Against a 3.35% CPI the real policy rate is now unambiguously **positive at ~+0.53pp** (and ~+0.54pp against core PCE). Inflation itself has not moved — August is still the latest print for every CPI/PPI gauge — and is still a producer-price story: **PPI all-commodities +9.85%** y/y (Jul +8.70%; m/m **+0.96%**, but 3m annualised **−3.1%**), CPI **+3.35%** y/y (Jul +3.30%; m/m **+0.40%**, 3m annualised only **+0.18%**), **core CPI just +2.45%** (Jul +2.47%; m/m +0.29%, 3m annualised **1.97%**), while **core PCE 3.34%** (Jul, 3m annualised 3.05%, m/m +0.25%; still no Aug print) and headline **PCE +3.70%** stay the hotter gauges. The two core gauges still disagree — but with crude retracing, the oil pass-through into core CPI that the 19-Sep read was waiting on now has less fuel behind it; **5y breakeven 2.34%** (23-Sep) says the market is not pricing one.
