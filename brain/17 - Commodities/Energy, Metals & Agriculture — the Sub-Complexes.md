# Energy, Metals & Agriculture — the Sub-Complexes

## Precious metals — the real-rate and fear complex

In the **Explorer catalog** gold and silver are positioning-only. The COT ingest is still stamped **2026-09-15** in the 2026-09-24 Explorer rebuild — no new CFTC report has landed — so these two rows are **re-verified, not restated**: `COT:GOLD` **+56.19% OI at the 99.1st percentile** of 872 weekly reports since 2010 (was +56.41%, 99.5th on 2026-09-08, and +53.19%, 97.7th on 2026-08-04); `COT:SILVER` **+24.41%, 59.9th percentile** (was +25.23%, 63.1st on 09-08, and +19.89%, 44.1st on 08-04).

**Prices have broken down while positioning has not.** The [Global Macro Trading Deck](../../macro%20deck/Global%20Macro%20Trading%20Deck.html) was recomputed on **2026-09-24** to a same-day observation (`meta.asof` 2026-09-24): **gold $4,288.50 (+12.39% YoY)** and **silver $63.985 (+44.79% YoY)** — against gold $4,424.90 / +19.02% and silver $66.556 / +59.52% at the deck's 2026-09-18 observation, and gold $4,481.70 / +24.04% in its 2026-09-08 build. Gold is now **−8.65% on the month** and the deck flags it *BELOW both* moving averages at the **35.7th** percentile of its own trailing year, down from 51.2nd; silver is **−6.78% on the month**, still *MIXED*, at the **38.5th** percentile, down from 48.8th. So the read this page carried on 2026-09-19 — "both metals are up hard on the year but sitting mid-range within it" — has resolved downward: both are now in the **lower** third of their own year, and gold has lost its trend. The melt-up is twelve months old and is currently unwinding.

Silver's "specs have not chased it" read from August is now only half true: the price is still +44.8% YoY at the deck's 2026-09-24 read, but positioning has climbed from the 44th percentile (08-04) to the 60th (09-15; it touched the 63rd on 09-08). Gold is the crowded one — and it is the one that has broken.

The catalog gap is still worth closing (gold is one of the assets the trade engine actively sizes), and it remains a **routing gap, not a data gap** — with the caveat, re-tested on 2026-09-24 and still true: FRED no longer serves the LBMA gold/silver fix series (`GOLDPMGBD228NLBM` and `SLVPRUSD` both return HTTP 400 today), so the deck is the only *dated* price source here (Macro COT Trades also pulls Yahoo `GC=F`/`SI=F` for sizing, but its output file carries no observation date). There is no FRED fallback for precious-metal spot.

The book's driver framing for precious metals — real rates and central-bank demand — *is* observable, and it has now delivered. The 10-year TIPS real yield is **2.76%** (`DFII10`, 2026-09-23) — a genuine fresh high for this cycle, the highest print since **2008-11-25** (2.79%) on a pull of the full 5,936-observation series. The September path is noisier than a single number suggests: 2.43% on 09-08, 2.60% on 09-14, 2.62% on 09-15, **2.68%** on 09-16, 2.61% on 09-17, **2.68%** on 09-18, 2.62% on 09-21, 2.63% on 09-22 and **2.76%** on 09-23 — so the 2.63% this page carried before was only the fourth-highest print of the month, and the high is the 09-23 one. It was 2.40% when this page was read on 2026-08-07; the Explorer's weekly `RATES:TIPS10` still stamps 2.43% at 2026-09-04 in its 2026-09-24 rebuild, so quote the daily series. The policy leg has moved too and has stayed moved: the Fed's target range is 3.75–4.00% (effective 2026-09-17) and daily fed funds has printed 3.88% every day since. A real yield above 2.6% is historically restrictive for a non-yielding asset — and **this refresh is the one where the price finally answered**: gold −8.65% on the month and below both moving averages, while specs stay pinned at +56.19% OI / 99th percentile. Rising real rates, a hiking Fed and record-crowded longs is the configuration the book's Ch 5 treats as reversal risk; on 2026-09-19 this page called it "materially tighter than a month ago", and the reversal has now started in the price with the crowd still in the trade. That is the dangerous half of the setup, not the resolved one.

Implied vol at the deck's 2026-09-24 observation: **GVZ 22.77** (16.3rd 1-yr percentile, 20-day realised 19.77, IV/RV 1.15 → *normal sizing*) and **VXSLV 41.88** (15.5th 1-yr percentile, realised 34.95, IV/RV 1.23 → *normal sizing*) — against GVZ 23.31 / 18.3rd / 1.07 and VXSLV 40.98 / 14.7th / 1.16 at the 2026-09-18 observation. Both sit near the bottom of their own year even as gold breaks down, so implied vol is **not** yet paying for the reversal risk above; at this read none of the three commodity vols is in cut-size territory.
