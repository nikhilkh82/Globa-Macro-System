# Endo-Exo Toolkit & Workflow

**What it is & why it matters** — This page documents the *working spreadsheet toolkit* built alongside the wiki to run the Global Macros FX method end-to-end, plus the maintained macro dataset that feeds it. The wiki pages describe the *method* on the historical teaching corpus; these tools apply that method to **live data** (through **August–September 2026** on the FRED-fed series as of the 2026-09-19 refresh; a few country-workbook drivers lag further — flagged where they matter). Keep the two mentally separate — see the vintage note at the bottom. The method itself is on [[FX Endogenous-Exogenous Framework]]; the broader process on [[The Global Macros Framework]] and [[Trade Idea Generation Process]].

## The workflow (how the pieces combine)

1. **Stage 1 — score each currency's endogenous drivers.** Run the [[The Global Macros Framework|endo]] template for the base and the quote currency. Each yields an absolute score: positive = that currency's purchasing power is debasing faster (inflationary). *Worked example:* the US endo, on live June-2026 data, scores **+27 ("Mildly Inflationary")** — cooled from +49 on course-vintage data by a record-low [[Leading Indicators|consumer-sentiment]] print and a [[Coincident Indicators|PPI]] spike.
2. **Stage 2 — score the relative (exogenous) drivers.** Run `Exogenous_Pair_Template.xlsx` for the pair: relative GDP growth, relative balance of payments, the interest-rate differential / carry, and relative equity returns — each scored from the base currency's perspective (+ favours base). Sum = EXO score → directional bias. *Worked example (course data):* **AUD/USD → −2 −6 −4 −2 = −14 → SHORT AUD/USD**, dominated by Australia's commodity-export reliance on Chinese demand. *Refreshed 2026-07-03* (`Exogenous_AUD_USD.xlsx`, fed by the US & AU endo workbooks): **−2 +2 +2 −6 = −4 → LOW-conviction short** — the BOP (AU CA −2.2% vs US −3.8%) and carry (RBA 4.31% vs Fed 3.63%) have *flipped AUD-positive* since the course era; the one dominant AUD-negative is relative equities (S&P +20% vs ASX +3% YoY). Agrees with the cockpit's exo (−6).
3. **Cross-check endo vs exo.** The higher-endo (faster-debasing) currency should be the weaker one. When the endo differential AGREES with the exo call, conviction is higher; when it CONFLICTS — or when [[Commitment of Traders (COT)]] positioning is crowded the other way — size the trade down. This is the deliberate tension the Global Macros process surfaces.
4. **Process the trade idea.** Feed the directional bias into [[Trade Idea Generation Process]] — quantitative screening ([[Average True Range (ATR)]], [[Distribution of Returns]]), catalysts, then [[Risk Management]] for stops and Kelly-based sizing.

## Scoring at a glance
- **Endo (stage 1):** ~16 domestic drivers, each −10…+10, summed on a −150…+160 scale via Z-score of the latest reading vs the indicator's full historical distribution.
- **Exo (stage 2):** 4 relative drivers, each −10…+10, summed on a −40…+40 scale; positive = long BASE/QUOTE.
- Both templates auto-recalculate on data entry; both are heatmap-styled (red→green) and carry on-sheet guidance.
